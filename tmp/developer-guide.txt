Workflow Clarity and Closure Developer
Guide

Implementation requirements from the platform walkthrough review

Purpose. This guide converts the walkthrough review into a focused implementation scope. It does not
redesign the existing platform. It makes document hand-offs clearer, reduces first-use complexity and
completes the operational closing flow from an accepted Sales Order through invoice, IRN, dispatch, E-way
Bill, payment and inventory debit.

Delivery objective

A user must be able to open any work item and immediately understand: what the document is, its current
status, who owns the next action, what needs to happen next, whether stock has moved and whether
available cash has changed.

In scope

· A shared document-status strip for Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Proformas, Sales Invoices, Purchase
     Invoices, GRNs and Dispatch Orders.

· Progressive disclosure in dense Purchase Order and Dispatch Order forms.
· Clearer role queues and a common queue record layout.
· The complete post-customer-acceptance sales execution flow, including manual IRN and E-way Bill stages for

     now.
· Idempotent task creation, notification events, audit history and acceptance tests.

Out of scope

· A direct government-portal or Tally integration. IRN and E-way Bill values remain manually recorded in this
     release.

· Changing current commercial approval policy. The implementation must use the approval rules already
     configured for the tenant.

· Changing historical stock, invoice or payment records.

4 Notifications and audit trail

· Create an in-app notification and email notification when a new task is assigned to a role or user.
· Record a timeline event for every creation, submission, approval, return, acceptance, compliance number

     entry, payment record, dispatch confirmation, cancellation and reversal.
· Timeline events must show timestamp, acting user, action, previous status, new status and linked reference

     where applicable.
· Email should contain document reference, counterparty, amount if relevant, next action and a direct platform

     link. It must not include sensitive bank details.

Manual compliance behaviour for this release

· IRN is manually entered by Finance against the approved or issued final invoice. Validate exactly 64
     alphanumeric characters. Do not generate a placeholder automatically.

· E-way Bill number is manually entered by Finance only after warehouse dispatch details are complete. Store
     number, date, valid-until value and supporting attachment where available.

· The compliance timeline must show who entered or changed the number. A correction must retain the previous
     value in audit history.

· Future Tally integration will replace manual capture. Invoice and dispatch data will be entered once in
     WhizUnik, transmitted through Tally and returned with IRN and E-way Bill results. Keep integration hooks
     separate from the current manual workflow.

Definition of done

· The status strip and queue layout work consistently across the listed operational documents.
· Dense forms use required-first sections without removing existing fields or document-output data.
· All four payment-term routes complete correctly: credit, 100 percent advance, part advance plus balance and

     goods in transit.
· IRN and E-way Bill manual capture, task gating, audit history and validation are complete.
· Physical dispatch creates exactly one stock debit and Treasury records actual cash only when payment is

     confirmed.
· The acceptance-test scenarios are verified in a clean test tenant and recorded with screenshots or test

     evidence.
